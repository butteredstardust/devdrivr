# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's identifying or
  security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling,
personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information
without permission are not acceptable. Sustained disruption or retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abuse on GitHub through
[GitHub's private reporting channels](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
For project-specific concerns, contact the maintainer privately using the
contact methods on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/butteredstardust). Do not put
sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as possible. They may
edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Retaliation against
a reporter is prohibited. Conflicts of interest will be disclosed and the
affected maintainer will step aside where possible.
